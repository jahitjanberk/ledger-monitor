About Ledger Monitor

Twelve dashboards on the systems people rely on every day: undersea cables, housing, scams, water, electricity, shipping routes, orbit, the air, food, public debt, heat and the minerals in our devices. Each one starts from a single finding and lets you check it.

How the figures are chosen

  • Every figure comes from a named publisher, and every one on the page links to its source.
  • Official statistics come first. Where there are none, survey or modelled estimates are used, and the source drawer says which kind each number is.
  • Reported and estimated figures are kept apart rather than added together, because they measure different things.
  • Each dashboard ends with a method note, a list of what it doesn’t show, and every source it uses.
  • All the data is published as CSV and JSON, with the source of each row, under the licences listed on GitHub.

Who makes it

Ledger Monitor is made by jahitjanberk. The code is MIT-licensed and lives on GitHub, along with the full methodology.

If a number is wrong or out of date, open an issue with a link to a better source. Corrections are recorded in the log below.
